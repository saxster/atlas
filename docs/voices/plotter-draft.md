# Creator Voice: Plotter Draft (`plotter-draft`)

**Register:** Creative Atelier (`quirk: 'atelier'`) / Pure 2D Orthographic Technical Drafting (`@saxster/atlas/draft_kit.js`, `engine/draft_standard.json`, generated tokens in `engine/draft_standard.js`).

Use `plotter-draft` whenever a lesson or engineering diagram is best told as a flat, high-discipline **2D orthographic construction plate** rather than a 3D perspective stage — linkages, structural sections, optical bench layouts, tolerance studies, timing mechanisms, or symbolic architectural constructions.

## Core Design Discipline

1. **Hierarchy through opacity, not stroke thickness noise:** Primary silhouettes, secondary details, leaders, guides, grid lines, and hatch rules share a uniform `1px` pen weight (`--atlas-stroke-primary: 1`) and separate through calibrated opacity tokens generated by `tools/build-standard.mjs`.
2. **Tip-first dashed transfer arrows (`draftArrow`):** Draw dashed transfer shafts `M<tip>L<start>` so the stroke-dasharray pattern starts on phase `0` at the arrowhead vertex, ensuring every open `45°` (`6` unit) arrowhead lands cleanly on a solid ink segment.
3. **Witness-derived split dimensions (`draftDimension`):** Derive dimension lines, gaps (`8`), overshoots (`8`), diagonal tick slashes (`8`), and label clearance breaks (`label.clearance`) mathematically from feature witnesses rather than placing manual lines.
4. **Clipped hatching & one-sided support ticks (`draftHatch`, `draftSupportTicks`):** Clip H1 (`-45°`) and H2 (`+45°`) diagonal hatching analytically to declared boundary rectangles; place S1 ground/wall hatch ticks one-sided with consistent direction (`side: ±1`).
5. **Non-destructive occlusions (`draftInverseClip`, `draftClearanceMask`):** Never fake occlusions with opaque paper-colored cover patches that break across themes. Use `clip-rule="evenodd"` inverse clip paths or `mask-type: luminance` masks instead.
6. **Coordinate-preserving compact views (`draftCompactSvg`) & container-query LOD:** Derive miniature thumbnail/card views by cropping `viewBox` + stripping text/dimension clutter and adding `vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke"` + `2 2` dashes (`--atlas-compact-dash`), preserving exact user coordinates.
7. **Non-boosting mark isolation (`draftIsolationWrap`, `bindDraftIsolation`):** Focus one group (`data-draft-focus="true"`) on hover, keyboard focus, or toggle click by fading unrelated elements to opacity `0` while keeping the selected stroke weights and opacities untouched.
8. **Anti-icon rule:** Every diagram must explain quantitative structure, spatial kinematics, or causal mechanisms — never draw decorative icon silhouettes, drop-shadows, gradients, blur filters, skew transforms, bold/italic chrome, or all-caps filler labels.

## Lint, Sanitize & Multi-Theme Raster Proof

- Lint strict orthographic rules: `npx atlas svg-lint <file...> --strict-draft`
- Lint compact viewports: `npx atlas svg-lint <file...> --compact`
- Export sanitized static SVG with resolved tokens: `npx atlas export-svg <in.svg> --out <out.svg> --theme light|dark|atelier`
- Inspect light & dark PNG renders: `npx atlas svg-render <in.svg> --out <out.png> --theme both`

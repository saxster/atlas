# Atlas of Mathematics (@saxster/atlas)

> trigonometry, calculus, linear algebra, differential equations and Fourier series

## Interactive Figures & Chapters

- **The unit circle** (`maths/unit-circle/`): sine and cosine as shadows
- **Slope and area** (`maths/calculus/`): derivatives, Riemann sums and the Fundamental Theorem
- **Linear transformations** (`maths/linear-algebra/`): matrices as grid moves, area scale and eigenvectors
- **Differential equations** (`maths/differential-equations/`): slope fields, phase portraits, spirals and saddles
- **Fourier series** (`maths/fourier/`): spinning epicycles, spectrum and the Gibbs ears
- **Multivariable calculus** (`maths/multivariable/`): surfaces, gradients, saddles, divergence and curl
- **The 2×2 matrix sandbox** (`maths/matrix-sandbox/` · `sandbox`): drag basis columns, type a matrix and test probes
- **From 2D base circle to 3D cross-section dome** (`maths/cross-sections/` · `machine`): Cavalieri’s slice integral ∫ A(x) dx and pochè cutaways
